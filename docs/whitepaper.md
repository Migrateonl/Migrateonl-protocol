# MIGRATE Whitepaper

## 1. Vision and mission

**Vision.** A market view that is open the moment you arrive: no wallet prompt, no signup, no waiting. MIGRATE wants anyone watching Solana to see where attention and volume are moving, and to see it first.

**Mission.** Turn the fast, noisy edge of Solana into a calm, readable board; keep every claim on the page traceable to a recorded source; and give the MIGRATE coin a permanent front-row seat on that board.

Principles:

- **Live by default.** The board never depends on a launch event.
- **Recorded, not assumed.** Outside facts (contract address, social channels, data sources) live in a single register. Anything unconfirmed is shown as pending.
- **Honest failure.** When a source goes quiet, the page says so and keeps the last good numbers on screen.

## 2. Product

The site ranks trending Solana tokens by 24h volume from a public market feed, refreshes once a minute while visible, and pins the MIGRATE row above the field once its contract address is recorded. A ticker repeats the same data. Every address shown can be copied with one click.

## 4. Roadmap

**Phase I, Foundation.** Live market board, contract address slot, register of outside facts, public repository.

**Phase II, Signal.** Sharper filters, watchlists and alerts layered over the same feed; deeper pool and holder views for the MIGRATE coin.

**Phase III, Community.** Holder tools, open channels, a public workshop for ideas and a visible way to vote on what ships next.

**Phase IV, Expansion.** Additional data sources, partner integrations and wider coverage beyond the first board.
